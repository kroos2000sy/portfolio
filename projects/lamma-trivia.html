Lamma: Live Trivia Game

Lamma (Arabic for a casual gathering of friends) is real-time, Kahoot-style trivia built for groups of adults. One big screen hosts the round while everyone else joins from their own phone with a room code or QR scan. Scoring rewards speed and confidence over lucky guesses, and a bank of 650+ questions across 20 categories means the same group of friends can play again next month without hitting a repeat.

Node.js Express Socket.io node:sqlite Arabic-first · RTL

host holds the state
The big screen is the source of truth; phones are just input. The host client owns the room, the question order, and the timer over Socket.io. Players' phones only send an answer event and render their own score. That split is what makes joining as simple as scanning a QR code or typing a six-character room code, with no login for anyone.
speed over luck
Scoring rewards how fast you answer, not just whether you're right. Points scale down from 1000 to a 300 floor based on how much of the time window is left, plus a streak bonus of up to +50% for consecutive correct answers. A wrong answer costs nothing, so no one stops playing after one bad guess.
free hosting, stateless by design
The free host tier sleeps after 15 minutes idle, and node:sqlite is a local file that resets on every cold start. That's fine here on purpose: the question bank loads fresh from bundled JSON at boot instead of depending on a persisted database, so a sleep-and-wake cycle never loses content. For a portfolio piece, a good, free, always-there link matters more than persistence.
